About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science with a deep interest in algorithms, data, and systems thinking. Teaching has taught me patience, clarity, and reflection — skills that travel enhances.

Traveling allows me to step outside structured academic life and observe real-world systems: cultures, economies, and human behavior. Each journey adds depth to how I teach, think, and write.

This blog is a personal archive of those journeys — written slowly, thoughtfully, and honestly.
